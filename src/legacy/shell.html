AUTHORITY / MODEL BOUNDARY

Bike mass: 211 kg operating calibration + 75 kg rider. Unsprung groups: 25 kg front / 22 kg rear. Wheelbase 1.410 m. Fork: 127 mm travel, 17.6 kN/m. Rear: 130 mm travel, 28.4 kN/m progressive wheel rate. Compression/rebound damping comes from the existing Ducati V84 calibration. Both tires retain V5 finite-thickness outer/inner surfaces and closed cavity. In FULL BIKE mode the tires do not own sprung payload; suspension forces act on their dynamic axle hubs. Rear drive torque acts through a chain/sprocket moment into the swingarm coordinate. Rolling-road mode integrates wheel inertia and tire slip separately from the existing imposed longitudinal-g test. Road restitution is zero.
