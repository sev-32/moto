DUCATI 916

Fully rigged 1997 mesh on a pressure-coupled distributed-brush motorcycle tyre model. 90° L‑twin with true 270°/450° firing · six‑speed dog box · dry clutch · telescoping fork · single‑sided swingarm · live chain · steering damper.
Countersteer, articulated rider CoG, pressure-driven contact patches, progressive grip saturation, wheel-hop and tyre thermal state are simulated. A switchable simulation ABS assist is enabled by default for controllability; it is explicitly not presented as original 1997 equipment. V68 adds provenance-tagged parameter identification, disjoint calibration/holdout targets, power-plane accounting, pitch/load-transfer observability and historical performance profiles on top of the V67 physics observatory. Every sound is synthesized causally — hot-gas bidirectional exhaust waves, intake tract, desmodromic mechanicals, dry-clutch backlash and camera-space propagation. Nothing is sampled.
